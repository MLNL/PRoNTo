# PRoNTo Mailing List & Support

At present, PRoNTo does not have a dedicated person managing developments or providing support. For general enquiries or if you are interested in contributing, please contact **Prof. Janaina Mourao-Miranda** or **Prof. Christophe Phillips** directly — see the [Contributing guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for more details. Please note that response times may vary.

---

## Bug Reports & Questions

For bug reports, feature requests, and questions, please use the [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/MLNL/PRoNTo_public/issues). This allows the community to benefit from shared answers and makes it easier to track known issues.

When reporting a bug, please include:

1. Your MATLAB version (e.g. R2022b)
2. Your operating system and architecture (e.g. Windows 11 64-bit)
3. Your SPM version/date (SPM12 or SPM25)
4. A description of the steps to reproduce the issue
5. Any error messages from the MATLAB console
